== Device connectivity

- iOS 17 and later devices on Mac mini hosts in Standard mode now come back online on their own after they restart or reconnect. Previously, after a restart, a device could stay in an error state and remain unavailable until deviceConnect services were restarted. This included the restart that happens when deviceConnect turns on Developer Mode on a device.
- Devices also recover without a services restart after the USB cable is unplugged and plugged back in, after the USB hub loses power, after the device moves to another USB hub, and after the Mac mini host restarts.
- Lightning mode hosts, Android devices, and iOS 16 and earlier devices were not affected.
