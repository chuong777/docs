== Device passcode unlock

- iOS devices that use a custom numeric passcode now unlock when the power button wakes them during a manual session. Previously, the passcode was not entered, the device stayed on the lock screen for the rest of the session, and installing an application or taking any other action that requires an unlocked device failed while it remained locked. Returning the device to service required entering the passcode by hand on the device.
- Devices running iOS 15.4.1 and 16.7.10 now finish preparation and become available for sessions. Previously, two steps in the preparation flow repeated without completing: setting a device passcode relaunched *Settings* repeatedly, and on-device automation restarted in a loop.

_Applies to organizations in PIN passcode mode._
